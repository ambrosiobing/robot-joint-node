\node[umlnote, text width=52mm, anchor=north] at (0.2,-2.2)
  {What this wire settles: the rate. An instrument sampling at tens of
   microseconds counts 2000 edges a second and would show immediately that the
   board is running at reset speed, which is the failure a miscompiled clock
   produces.\\[3pt]
   What it cannot settle: the distribution. The jitter this chapter reports is
   measured in tens of nanoseconds, three orders of magnitude below this
   instrument's resolution. That measurement happens inside the part, with the
   cycle counter, and the report says which instrument produced which number.};

\node[chlabel, anchor=west] at (2.2,1.3) {no oscilloscope, no logic analyser, no probe};
