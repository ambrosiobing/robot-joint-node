   two-wire transaction takes hundreds of microseconds and can stall, and the
   handler has a deadline every millisecond.};
\node[umlnote, text width=56mm, anchor=north west] at (7.0,-10.2)

   own oscillator, whose tolerance is far looser than a crystal's, and over a
   deep buffer that difference is the whole error.};
